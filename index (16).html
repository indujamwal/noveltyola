FAQ

Questions we hear every day.

Can I charge an Ola at home?+

Yes. Ola scooters and motorcycles charge from a regular home socket with the charger provided. Most riders charge overnight, the same way they charge a phone.

How much does it cost to run an Ola?+

Usually a fraction of petrol. Use the savings calculator above with your own daily distance and electricity rate for an estimate.

Do you offer finance and EMI?+

Yes. We arrange finance with leading banks and NBFCs at the showroom. Bring your Aadhaar, PAN and address proof and we'll handle the rest.

Can I exchange my petrol scooter?+

Yes. Bring your old two-wheeler to any Novelty Ola outlet. We'll value it on the spot and adjust it against your new Ola.

What about the warranty on the battery?+

Battery and vehicle warranty depends on the model and variant you choose. Our team will walk you through the exact coverage before you book.

Where do I get my Ola serviced?+

Novelty Ola handles periodic service, software updates and repairs. Book a service through the form below or call us.
